# LawDesk AI — Corporate Legal & Regulatory Compliance RAG Bot

LawDesk AI automates CAMA 2020 statutory citations, NDPA 2023 compliance auditing, and contractual clause liability screening over WhatsApp and Telegram.

## Features
- **Statutory Retrieval**: Instant citations for CAC annual returns, single-member companies, and foreign investment capital repatriation.
- **Contract Risk Scanner**: Flags uncapped indemnities, consequential damages, and unilateral termination defects.
- **Retainer Booking**: Seamless consultation reservation with dual-currency Paystack/Stripe checkout.
